Give

A little every month goes further than you'd think.

A steady hundred dollars a month lets us plan years ahead for an animal — which is exactly the kind of promise elephant care requires. One-time gifts are wonderful too, and every single one helps. But monthly givers are the quiet heroes of this model.

Every gift is unrestricted unless you tell us otherwise, which means it goes wherever the care team says it's needed most — often the unglamorous things, like fencing and water pumps, that quietly make everything else possible.

Sponsorship

Choose one elephant, and stand behind her for years.

Sponsors adopt a named resident and receive her file four times a year: keeper reports, medical notes, habitat footage filmed with nobody around, and the parts of her story we don't share on the website. You'll know her better than almost anyone.

The one thing sponsorship can't include, at any level, is hands-on time with her — her retirement means no more meetings, poses or appearances, for anyone. We've found that the people drawn to sponsor an elephant here are exactly the people who love that about it.

  • Friend — $50 a month. Quarterly file and the annual welfare audit.
  • Guardian — $250 a month. The above, plus a monthly keeper letter and a members' evening invitation.
  • Patron — $1,000 a month. The above, plus a named plaque in the retirement barn and two cabin nights a year.

We cap the number of sponsors per resident, so every sponsor gets the full story, every quarter, properly told.

Partners & grants

Partners who fund the unglamorous things.

Corporate partnerships and grants underwrite the specific, essential things: the veterinary barn, the water system, the fencing program, the keeper training fund.

We're careful, too. We pass on partnerships that ask for elephant photo opportunities or events on the habitat side of the fence — and we say a grateful yes to organizations that want to fund real infrastructure and be thanked sincerely for it.

Talk to us
  • Named infrastructure — barn, wallow, water tower, boundary
  • Winnie’s Farm — the rescue barn, paddocks and feed program
  • Multi-year keeper training and salary underwriting
  • Veterinary equipment and senior-elephant care programs
  • Habitat expansion, acre by acre
  • Matched-giving campaigns for the endowment
Legacy giving

A gift that outlives all of us.

Our care endowment exists so that a bad year, a recession, or anything else the world throws at us never becomes an elephant's problem. We fund it before we spend surplus on anything else, and it's built to cover full care for the whole herd for three years, even with no income at all.

Gifts in wills, appreciated stock, donor-advised funds and charitable trusts all go to the endowment by default. And if you'd like to see exactly what your legacy would protect, we'll happily walk you through the model, line by line.

Transparency

We share everything, every year — the good and the hard.

Our annual accounts, the independent welfare audit, intake decisions and the full field log are all public. If we ever have a hard year with an animal, you'll hear it from us first, honestly and completely.
