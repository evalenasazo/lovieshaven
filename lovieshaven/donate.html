Support

Your gift buys her care. It does not buy you access to her.

The retreat covers the running of this place. Philanthropy is what lets us take the next elephant, and the one after that, and keep them all for thirty years.

The commitment

The remaining life we plan for with every intake. We do not accept an elephant we cannot fund to the end.

Give

Monthly is worth more to us than magnificent.

A predictable hundred dollars a month lets us plan a decade. A dramatic one-off lets us plan a fortnight. Both are welcome; only one changes what we can promise an animal.

Every gift is unrestricted unless you say otherwise, which means it goes wherever the care team says it is needed most — usually the least photogenic thing on the property.

Sponsorship

Stand behind one elephant, quietly, for years.

Sponsors adopt a named resident and receive her file four times a year: keeper reports, medical notes, habitat footage filmed with nobody present, and the parts of her history we do not put on the website.

What sponsorship does not include, at any level, is a visit with her, a meeting, a hand on her, or a photograph. She is not available, and no amount of money makes her available. Supporters who understand that are the reason this model works.

  • Friend — $50 a month. Quarterly file and the annual welfare audit.
  • Guardian — $250 a month. The above, plus a monthly keeper letter and a members' evening invitation.
  • Patron — $1,000 a month. The above, plus a named plaque in the retirement barn and two cabin nights a year.

Sponsorship of a resident is capped so that no elephant carries more supporters than we can report to properly.

Partners & grants

We are careful about whose name goes on this.

Corporate partnership and grant funding underwrite specific, unglamorous things: the veterinary barn, the water system, the fencing programme, the keeper training fund.

We decline partnerships that require elephant imagery in advertising, elephant contact for executives, or any activity on the habitat side of the fence. We say yes to organisations that want to fund infrastructure and be thanked in a footnote.

Talk to us
  • Named infrastructure — barn, wallow, water tower, boundary
  • Winnie’s Farm — the rescue barn, paddocks and feed programme
  • Multi-year keeper training and salary underwriting
  • Veterinary equipment and geriatric care programmes
  • Habitat expansion, acre by acre
  • Matched-giving campaigns for the endowment
Legacy giving

The endowment is the only thing here that outlives all of us.

Our care endowment exists so that a bad year, a recession or a closed border never becomes an elephant's problem. It is funded before any surplus is spent on anything else, and it is designed to cover full care for the whole herd for three years with no income at all.

Gifts in wills, appreciated stock, donor-advised funds and charitable trusts all go there by default. If you want to know exactly what a legacy gift would protect, we will show you the model line by line.

Transparency

Published every year, whether it flatters us or not.

Annual accounts, the independent welfare audit, intake decisions and the full field log are all public. If we ever have a bad year with an animal, you will read about it here first.
