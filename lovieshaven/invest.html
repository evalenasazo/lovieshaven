The Founding 150

Be one of the hundred and fifty who make this real.

Lovie’s Haven is a big dream — 150 acres, a herd, a pavilion, a village — and we want to be completely honest about what it costs. The full build is more than any single raise can cover. The Founding 150 is the part that has to come first: the land, and the sanctuary itself. And we're inviting exactly one hundred and fifty people to be the ones who did it.

The ask

$7.5 million from a hundred and fifty founders — the money that makes it real.

Here's the honest picture: the full estate — pavilion, bungalows, village — is a fifteen-to-twenty-million-dollar build, and no single raise was ever going to cover all of it. The Founding 150 funds the part everything else depends on.

Founding capital buys the land and builds the sanctuary itself — elephant-grade fencing, water, wallows, the barns, the farm — plus the first doors that earn: the café and the first village huts, open early so the herd's care has income from year one. We won't borrow against any of it — we never want a loan payment competing with an elephant's care budget. Once the land is ours and the herd's home is funded, we raise the rest the way institutions do: named gifts, grants, and partners who want their name on a building, phase by phase.

The Founding 150 opens once, fills once, and closes forever. After the hundred-and-fiftieth member joins, there will simply never be another — no matter what comes later.

Where the money goes

Phase One is the sanctuary — and the first doors that earn for it.

Every founding dollar goes first to the herd's side of the plan — the land under everything, and the things an elephant actually needs — and then to the first earning corners of the retreat, because the retreat is what pays for elephant care. We'll refine the budget against the final property, share it with founders line by line, and keep publishing the real numbers as we build.

Phase Two — the full Sky View Pavilion, the rest of the bungalows and huts, the village, the endowment — is the capital campaign that follows, funded by named gifts, grants and partners. That campaign becomes possible the day the founders put real land under it. That's the gift of going first.

The honest arithmetic

Why lifetime benefits don't bankrupt the sanctuary.

A promise of "free stays for life" is only worth making if the numbers truly work. Ours do, and we'd rather show you the whole calculation than ask you to take our word for it.

All hundred and fifty founders together use 300 room-nights a year. Our eleven cabins offer roughly four thousand. That means founders fill under eight percent of the estate's nights — the rest are sold to guests, and every sold night funds elephant care. Classes cost us nothing extra: the instructor is already teaching, and the room already faces the meadow. And the gala is one evening a year we'd be hosting anyway — there's simply nobody we'd rather host.

So founders aren't a cost this place carries. They're its foundation — a hundred and fifty people with a lifetime reason to love and protect it.

How it works

Five simple steps between this page and opening day.

1 · Reserve

Tell us which tier feels right. A reservation is just a conversation and a letter of intent — no money moves yet.

2 · Agree

Every founder signs the same written founding-membership agreement: your benefits and our obligations, spelled out in plain language.

3 · Escrow

Your funds are held safely in escrow until the raise completes and the land closes. If either doesn't happen, every dollar comes back to you.

4 · Build

You'll follow the build the way sponsors follow the field log: honestly and completely — budgets, actual costs, delays and all.

5 · Open

Your benefits begin opening week and never end. And so does the herd's retirement — the thing you really built.
