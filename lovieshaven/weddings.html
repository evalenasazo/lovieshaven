Weddings & gatherings

Marry beside a meadow where rescued elephants live free.

We host just twelve weddings a year, for up to 120 guests — and every single one helps fund a lifetime of elephant care. We keep them gentle and quiet out of love for the herd, and honestly, that's what makes them so beautiful. Weekend packages start at $15,000.

The setting

Glass, meadow, water, and a treeline that goes on.

Ceremonies are held on the Elephant View Meadow at the edge of the village — or, if the weather has other plans, inside the Sky View Pavilion with all three glass walls open to the light.

Dinner is long tables under the Sky View Pavilion roof, which seats 150 comfortably. The whole village is yours for the weekend: eleven cabins, twenty-two beds, the café, the spa rooms, the lake, and a team who will keep the whole celebration feeling unhurried.

And here's the part couples remember forever: there's a real chance the herd will be grazing the far side of the meadow while you say your vows. We'll never pose an elephant for your photos — but an elephant who simply chose to be there on your wedding day? That's a kind of magic you can't stage.

The conditions

A few promises we ask every couple to make with us.

Every wedding here follows guidelines written by our care team, because this is the elephants' home first. They're the same for every couple, at every budget — and they're the reason the herd has never once been disturbed by a wedding.

If you read this list and love this place even more, you're exactly the kind of couple we'd be honored to host.

  • No amplified music outdoors at any hour
  • Live acoustic only in the meadow, ending at 21:00
  • Sound monitored continuously at the habitat line; if it rises, the volume comes down
  • No fireworks, sparklers, drones, confetti cannons or vehicle horns
  • No lighting aimed toward the habitat after dusk
  • No access to the elephant side of the property for any person, at any time
  • Photograph the herd from where you stand; nobody leaves the trail for a closer shot
The weekend package — from $15,000

Friday afternoon to Sunday morning, built around one perfect Saturday.

The package includes the two waterfront bungalows on the lake — one for each side of the aisle, each sleeping up to four — and the Sky View Pavilion is all yours for the whole of Saturday.

The rest of the estate carries on quietly around you. Your wider party can add the remaining nine cabins, guests beyond that arrive by shuttle, and couples who want the whole place to themselves can book the full buyout — most land somewhere in between.

A planner is with you from the first call to the last car. And here's our favorite part: twenty percent of every wedding goes straight to the care endowment — and afterward, we'll tell you exactly which elephant it helped, and how.
