Wellness

A glass room on the treeline, and a rhythm you can fall into by the second morning.

The pavilion is the heart of everything guests do here. Glass on three sides, timber overhead, warm stone underfoot, and a horizon that occasionally has elephants on it.

Why people come

Rehabilitation is not a word we only use about elephants.

Most guests arrive carrying something specific: burnout, a grief that hasn't moved, anxiety that won't switch off, or a transition — a divorce, a diagnosis, a company sold, a parent buried — that nobody gave them time to absorb.

We do not treat any of that. We are not a clinic and we do not employ clinicians. What we offer is the same thing we offer the herd: space, quiet, an unhurried day, and no obligation to be anywhere or be anyone.

The daily rhythm

Six things a day. Attend all of them or none.

The schedule never changes and never depends on the herd. If elephants happen to be on the far field during your sunrise practice, that is a gift. If they aren't, the practice is identical.

Group sizes are capped at sixteen for movement and twenty-four for sound. Private instruction is available in the treatment rooms or on your own deck.

Beyond the pavilion

Treatment rooms, cold water, and a lot of nothing.

Bodywork

Deep tissue, lymphatic, craniosacral and myofascial release in three timber treatment rooms. Sixty or ninety minutes.

Heat and cold

Cedar sauna, cold plunge and a hot pool set into the rock above the south lake. Open dawn to curfew.

Breath and stillness

Guided breathwork on request, and a silent room with no windows, no clock and nothing to do in it.

For people who want this to be part of their year, not their holiday.

Retreats

Four nights, sixteen people, one long exhale.

Hosted retreats run eight times a year and sell out on the member list before they are announced publicly.

Each is built around a single theme — grief, rest, transition, creative work — and each includes the full practice programme, all meals, one treatment, a private evening with the care team, and four nights in a Treeline or Lake cabin.

From $4,900 per person

Private retreats

Companies, boards, foundations and families take the property whole. We build the schedule with you, hold your group to the same quiet hours as everyone else, and route ten per cent of the fee to the care endowment.

Popular with organisations who want a room full of people to leave caring about something.
