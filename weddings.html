Weddings & gatherings

Twelve weddings a year. A hundred and twenty guests. Silence by nine.

We host a small number of weddings because they fund a great deal of care — and we host them under conditions strict enough that the herd has never once reacted to one.

The setting

Glass, meadow, water, and a treeline that goes on.

Ceremonies are held in the meadow at the edge of the village or, in weather, inside the pavilion with all three glass walls open to the light.

Dinner is long tables under the pavilion roof, which seats a hundred and fifty comfortably. The whole village is yours for the weekend: eleven cabins, twenty-two beds, the café, the spa rooms, the lake, and a team who will keep the entire thing feeling unhurried.

What you will not get is an elephant in your photographs. That is not a limitation we apologise for — it is the reason this place is worth marrying in.

The conditions

Read these before you fall in love with the meadow.

Every wedding here operates inside a set of limits written by the care team, not the events team. They are not negotiable at any budget, and we would rather lose a booking than bend one.

Couples who read this list and feel more certain rather than less are exactly the couples we want.

  • No amplified music outdoors at any hour
  • Live acoustic only in the meadow, ending at 21:00
  • Sound monitored continuously at the habitat line; if it rises, the volume comes down
  • No fireworks, sparklers, drones, confetti cannons or vehicle horns
  • No lighting aimed toward the habitat after dusk
  • No access to the elephant side of the property for any person, at any time
  • No photography of the herd, by anyone, on your day
What's included

One weekend, one team, nothing outsourced.

The property

Exclusive use for two or three nights. Eleven cabins, the glass pavilion, the meadow, the café, the spa rooms and the lake.

The table

Our kitchen, our cellar, our long tables. Welcome dinner, ceremony service, reception and a slow farewell breakfast.

The weekend

Sunrise practice for anyone who wants it, treatments by appointment, a keeper's talk for your guests, and a planner from first call to last car.

Twenty per cent of every wedding fee goes to the care endowment and is reported to you afterwards, by name, with what it paid for.
